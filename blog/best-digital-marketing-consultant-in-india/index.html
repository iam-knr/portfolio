What a digital marketing consultant does

A digital marketing consultant advises on, and often directly runs, the channels that bring you customers online: search ads, social ads, SEO, content, email, WhatsApp and analytics. The role is part strategist, part operator.

Usual work includes:

  • Auditing your current marketing and tracking.
  • Setting goals and a budget plan tied to revenue.
  • Building and running campaigns, or guiding the person who does.
  • Fixing the funnel: landing pages, forms, follow up.
  • Reporting in business terms.
  • Training your team.

In 2026 a strong consultant also knows how to use AI to speed up research, creative, reporting and follow up, and can tell you honestly where AI is not worth the trouble.

Many good setups mix them: a consultant sets direction and checks quality, while an agency or freelancers execute.

10 questions to ask

  1. Which campaigns have you run in my category? Category knowledge shortens the learning period.
  2. Can you show an account or report with real numbers? Names can be hidden. Numbers should not be vague.
  3. What would you check first in my account? The answer should be tracking and conversion setup, before any new campaign.
  4. How do you measure success? Look for qualified leads, revenue and payback, not only clicks.
  5. What do you need from me? Access, creative approvals, sales feedback.
  6. Who will do the daily work?
  7. What is your view on automation and AI? A good answer includes limits and approvals.
  8. How do you report, and how often?
  9. What would make you say this will not work for us? Honest consultants have an answer.
  10. Can we begin with a paid audit or a short trial?

Red flags

  • Guaranteed rankings, "page one in 30 days" or fixed return on ad spend.
  • Will not give you account ownership. Ad accounts, analytics and domains must be yours.
  • No questions about your margins, sales process or customer value.
  • Reports full of impressions and followers, nothing about money.
  • A long lock in contract before any proof.
  • Pressure to spend more before fixing basics.
  • Case studies you cannot verify.

Pricing models in India

Prices vary by city, experience and scope, so I will not give a single range. Here are the models and what to check for each.

ModelHow it worksWatch out for
Hourly or day ratePay for time, usually for audits and trainingScope creep. Fix the list of what you get
Monthly retainerFixed fee for set hours or tasksVague tasks. Write them down
Project feeOne price for a defined build, such as an audit or funnel setupUnclear end point
Percentage of ad spendFee tied to spendEncourages higher spend, not better results
Performance linkedPart of fee tied to resultsNeeds clean tracking and agreed definitions

Ad spend itself is separate. You pay the platforms directly from your own account.

What good results look like in the first 90 days

  • Days 1 to 14. Audit, tracking fixes, goal and budget plan agreed.
  • Days 15 to 45. Campaigns and landing pages live. First learning report with real findings.
  • Days 46 to 90. Cost per qualified lead trending down or revenue per rupee trending up, with a written reason for each change.

If you see nothing but activity reports at day 60, ask hard questions.

How to run your own selection

  1. Write your goal in one sentence with a number and a date.
  2. Shortlist two or three people. Check live work and reviews you can verify.
  3. Hold a 30 minute call with each. Use the ten questions above.
  4. Ask each for a short written first step and its price.
  5. Start with a paid audit or a 30 to 60 day trial.
  6. Review at day 30 with real numbers.

SOP: The first paid audit, step by step

A paid audit is the best first step with any consultant. It limits your risk and shows you how they think. Here is what a good one covers and in what order.

SOP 15: Marketing audit Owner: consultant with your account lead. Time: 5 to 10 working days

  1. Kickoff call. Goals, margins, sales process, past tests. Ask who your best customer is and how they found you.
  2. Access. View access to ad accounts, analytics, tag manager, CRM and the website. Accounts stay in your name.
  3. Tracking audit. Make test conversions. Confirm each is recorded once. Compare ad platform numbers with CRM numbers.
  4. Funnel review. Traffic, form fills, qualified leads, sales. Note the biggest drop.
  5. Campaign review. Structure, budgets, audiences, keywords, creative, search terms, placements.
  6. Landing page review. Message match, speed, form, trust signals, mobile layout.
  7. Follow up review. Time to first reply, scripts, WhatsApp and email flows, CRM stages.
  8. Competitor and market check. Ad libraries, search results, pricing pages.
  9. Write the findings. Each finding has evidence, impact, effort and owner.
  10. Present and prioritize. Top three fixes for the first 30 days with expected effect.
Output: a written report, a ranked fix list and a 30 day plan. You keep the report even if you do not hire them for the next step.

An audit report outline to ask for

  1. Summary on one page: three findings, three fixes, expected effect.
  2. Tracking status with screenshots and test results.
  3. Funnel numbers for the last 90 days.
  4. Channel by channel review with specific issues.
  5. Landing page and follow up review.
  6. Budget view: where money goes and what it returns.
  7. Risks and unknowns, in plain words.
  8. The 30, 60 and 90 day plan.

If a proposed audit is a list of generic tips that could apply to anyone, ask for the version that uses your numbers.

SOP: Review a monthly report together

SOP 16: Monthly review meeting Owner: you and your consultant. Time: 45 minutes

  1. Read the one page summary before the call. Mark what you do not understand.
  2. Check the headline number. Cost per qualified lead or revenue per rupee spent. Compare with the target.
  3. Ask why. For any big change, ask for the cause in the data, not a guess.
  4. Review last month's actions. What was done, what happened.
  5. Decide next month's tests. One to three, each with a metric and a day to decide.
  6. Agree the budget. Changes with reasons, written down.
  7. Write the notes and send them the same day.
Output: written decisions for the month. If the report is only impressions and followers, ask for the money numbers.

Worked example: what a fair first month could look like

Illustrative example. Not a client result.

A clinic spends a fixed monthly amount on search and social ads. It gets leads but few appointments.

  • Week 1 audit. Conversion tracking counts button clicks as leads, inflating the numbers. The form sends to an inbox that nobody checks on weekends.
  • Week 2 fixes. Track real form submits. Add a phone click event. Route enquiries to a shared number with a weekend rota.
  • Week 3 tests. Rewrite the landing page headline to match the main search term. Shorten the form to name, phone and treatment.
  • Week 4 review. The reported lead count drops because it is now accurate. The share of leads who book rises because replies are faster.

Notice that the first win was honest numbers, not more spend. A consultant who starts there is usually serving you well.

What you should own at the end

If a consultant resists any of these, treat that as a signal. Good professionals are happy to leave you in control.

How AI changes the work, and what stays the same

AI makes research, reporting and first drafts faster. It does not choose your offer, understand your customers' real fears, or know your margins. A strong consultant uses AI where it helps, shows you where, and tells you what they still check by hand. Ask for examples, such as "which task did you stop doing by hand last year, and what do you still check yourself?"

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best digital marketing consultant in India?

There is no single best for every business. The best fit has run campaigns in your category, can show real results, ties work to qualified leads or revenue, and agrees to start with a paid audit or trial. Kailas (KNR) Nath R is one option, especially for ads, funnels and AI automation.

What does a digital marketing consultant charge in India?

It depends on experience, scope and model: hourly, retainer, project, percentage of spend or performance linked. Ask for the list of what you get and compare two or three quotes. Ad spend is paid separately to the platforms.

Do I need a consultant or an agency?

Choose a consultant for strategy, audits and senior guidance. Choose an agency when you need many channels run daily. Many businesses use a consultant to guide an agency or in house team.

How do I verify a consultant's results?

Ask to see anonymised account screenshots with dates, talk to a past client, and check their public work such as articles, courses, code or plugins. Be careful with claims you cannot check.

How long before I see results?

Paid ads can show early signals in a few weeks. SEO usually takes months. Expect clear findings and fixes in the first month and trends in cost per qualified lead by month three.

Should my consultant use AI?

Yes where it saves time or improves quality, such as research, reporting and follow up. They should also explain its limits and keep approvals on spending and customer messages.
