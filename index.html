About · the long version

The guy your competitors hope you never hire.

I am Kailas Nath R. Most people call me KNR. For 7+ years I have lived inside ad accounts, doing the quiet work that makes somebody's competitor nervous.

I run campaigns on search, social, programmatic and marketplaces for enterprise and retail brands. That is the day job and it comes first. The teaching came later, mostly because people kept asking how I did it.

I also write code. When a marketing job gets repetitive I get bored. And when I get bored, I build an agent to do it for me. That habit has turned into plugins, tools and one full product so far.

House rules

How I work

Rule 01

Tracking before budget

I do not spend a rupee until I trust the numbers. Most accounts I open have a tracking problem nobody noticed.

Rule 02

Real accounts only

I do not teach anything I have not run myself. If it has not survived a live campaign, it stays out of my class.

Rule 03

Boring work goes to machines

If a person does the same task three times, I start writing an agent for it. People should do the thinking.

Rule 04

No fairy tales

I tell you what the numbers say, even when you will not enjoy hearing it. Perhaps that is the villain part.
