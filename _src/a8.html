What an agentic AI consultant does

An agentic AI consultant helps a business use AI agents to do real work. A good one does five things.

  1. Finds the right tasks. Not every task should have an agent.
  2. Designs the system. Goal, tools, data, limits, approvals.
  3. Builds it. Often in a workflow tool such as n8n with MCP connections, or in code.
  4. Tests and secures it. With real data, edge cases and logs.
  5. Trains your team and hands over. So you are not locked in.

A consultant is not a reseller of a chatbot subscription. If the offer is "we install a tool and send a bill each month", that is a vendor, not a consultant.

9 questions to ask before you hire

  1. Can I see an agent you built that is running now? A live system or a recorded walkthrough with real data. Not slides.
  2. What did it do wrong, and how did you fix it? People who have built agents have stories. People who have not, do not.
  3. What can the agent do without a human? Good answer: read, draft, report. Spending and messaging customers need approval at first.
  4. How do you stop runaway loops and costs? Step limits, spend caps, timeouts, alerts.
  5. Where does my data go? Which model provider, which server, what is stored, what is logged. Ask about India's DPDP Act, 2023.
  6. How will we know it works? A number agreed before the build: hours saved, response time, cost per qualified lead.
  7. Who owns the build? You should own the workflows, prompts and documentation.
  8. What happens when a tool or API changes? Ask about maintenance and who is on call.
  9. Can we start with a small paid pilot? A good consultant says yes and defines it in two to four weeks.

Red flags

  • Promises like "10x revenue" or "fully autonomous marketing".
  • No live example, only logos.
  • No mention of testing, approvals or logs.
  • Pushes one tool for every problem.
  • Long lock in with no pilot.
  • Cannot explain the design in plain words.
  • Reviews and case studies that cannot be checked.

What drives cost

I will not quote a made up price range. These are the items that decide the price. Ask the consultant to price each one separately.

  • Scope. Number of tasks and tools connected.
  • Data readiness. Messy CRMs and missing tracking add work.
  • Risk level. Systems that touch money or customers need more testing.
  • Hosting and model usage. Often paid by you directly at cost.
  • Training and handover. Documentation, videos, a live session.
  • Support. Monthly hours or a retainer for fixes and changes.

A sensible first project

A good pilot has a clear end and a clear number.

  • Task: lead response and routing. New enquiry arrives, agent checks and scores it, sends first reply, alerts sales.
  • Safe mode first: drafts replies for approval for two weeks.
  • Number: time to first reply, and share of leads reaching a sales call.
  • Length: two to four weeks.
  • Decision: keep, widen or stop, based on the numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best agentic AI consultant in India?

No honest list can name one best for everyone. Choose the one who can show live agents like yours, explains safety and approvals clearly, and agrees to a small paid pilot with a measurable goal. Kailas (KNR) Nath R is one option for marketing and ads automation.

What does an agentic AI consultant do?

They choose suitable tasks, design the agent system, build and test it, set safety limits, connect it to your tools and train your team.

How much does an agentic AI consultant cost in India?

It depends on scope, data readiness, risk, hosting, training and support. Ask for each priced separately and start with a pilot of two to four weeks.

How long does it take to build an AI agent?

A read-only reporting or audit agent can take days. An agent that takes actions with approvals and several integrations usually takes a few weeks including testing.

Should I hire a consultant or build in house?

If the task is simple and low risk, learn and build it yourself. If it touches money, customers or many systems, a consultant can prevent costly mistakes. A hybrid works well: consultant builds, your team learns.

What is the difference between an AI consultant and an agentic AI consultant?

An AI consultant may advise on strategy or tools broadly. An agentic AI consultant specialises in systems where AI plans and acts across tools to reach a goal.
